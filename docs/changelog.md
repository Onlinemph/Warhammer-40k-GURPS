# Changelog

Anchor and rule changes to `framework.md`, with the reason for each.

## Wave 1 (core Imperium)

- **Power-field rule added.** All disruption-field weapons use armour divisor (10). Drafts had thunder hammer (3), power fist (5), chainfist (6); at (3) a Marine's average thunder-hammer blow couldn't penetrate Terminator armour, the opposite of its role in the fiction.
- **Meltagun beyond ½D** is 4d×3(5), not bare dice with no divisor. The draft made a meltagun weaker than a lasgun at 15 yards.
- **Autogun anchor added at 4d+1 pi** (lasgun parity). The flak line said flak "stops stub rounds", which pushed the draft autogun to 2d+2 pi; reworded to pistol-calibre rounds.
- **TL bracket for support weapons** (mortar TL6, autocannon/missile launcher TL7–8).
- **"Senior agents" point tier (350–550)** for field Inquisitors and equivalents.
- **Astartes physiology:** Ossmodula grants DR 3 torso/vitals (fused ribcage); racial attribute block adds HP +4, FP +3. Package 293 → 323 points.
- **Force weapons use Broadsword/Staff/Axe skills**, not Force Sword (the GURPS beam-blade skill). Force strike adds +1d per level of Psyker Talent.
- **Psychic powers rebuilt on the Thaumatology: Sorcery model** (user direction: psychic powers use the Thaumatology: Sorcery model). Added Warp Empowerment (Sorcerous Empowerment reskin, TSOR4, 10+10/level) as the trait that gates which powers a psyker may know by point cost, mapped to the Adeptus Astra Telepathica's Assignment grades; Psyker (Talent) is now explicitly the Sorcery Talent analogue (TSOR5, 10/level, skills-only); the old bespoke -15% "Warp" power modifier became "Warp Sorcery" at -20% (Sorcery -15% + Detectable -5%, TSOR); Perils of the Warp is unchanged and stacks on top as the setting's own risk. Every ability in Imperium/Traits/Psykers and the Warp.adq and all four psyker templates were updated to match; fixed two pre-existing grade/Talent-level mismatches found in the process (Astropath's "Beta-grade" text said Talent 3 while the Assignment table said Beta is Talent 4; the Librarian add-on used a Gamma-equivalent Talent 4 while the table names Astartes Librarians as Alpha).
- **Personal force fields are regenerating shields** (user direction: Halo-style shields). Shield Points absorb damage before armour and recharge after a short delay; replaces the earlier flat-DR fields.
- **Inquisitorial authority** is modelled as Legal Enforcement Powers + Inquisitorial Rank (overrides all Imperial hierarchies, +100%) + Status (user decision). Inquisitor 474 → 559, Interrogator 326 → 366; senior-agents tier widened to 350–600.

## Rescale (user direction)

- **Every damage and armour anchor raised** to sit above GURPS's real-world baseline (modern plate ~DR 30–35, 5.56 mm ~5d pi). The first pass had Astartes power armour at DR 40, barely above a modern plate carrier. New anchors: flak 12, carapace 45, Sororitas 70, Astartes Mk VII 100 (the user's own table uses 86; they chose 100 for harder penetration), Gravis 130, Terminator 200; boltgun 6d×2(2) pi++ (user minimum); vehicles AV10–14 = DR 100/150/200/275/350; shields doubled. Lore matchups (lasgun vs flak, bolter vs Marine, plasma vs Terminator, lascannon vs tank) are re-checked at the new scale.
- **Astartes ST 18 → 30** (user direction), HP 22 → 34; Neophyte ST 15 → 24. Keeps a single penetrating bolt (avg ~40 with follow-up) survivable at the new damage scale. Racial package 323 → 443 points.
- **Weapon handling rules added** (user feedback: RoF 3 and ST 11 had been copied across weapons without reason, and Astartes boltguns needed only ST 11). Framework now derives weight, min ST, RoF, Shots, Bulk, Recoil and Acc per weapon.
- **Human-scale bolt weapons** use pi instead of pi++ and a smaller follow-up (boltgun 3d, pistol 2d cr ex) (user direction). Melee handling stats audited alongside ranged.
- **Guard armour tougher and lasgun 7d(2)** (user direction): flak 12 → 18 (helmet 15), mesh 15 → 22, carapace 45 → 60; laspistol 4d(2), lasgun 7d(2), long-las 8d(2), hellgun 8d(3), autogun 7d pi. Lasgun vs carapace stays about 9%.
- **Flak 30, carapace 70** (user direction; mesh 36, flak helmet 25, scout armour 77). **Sororitas power armour 70 → 85** so it stays above carapace. Lasgun vs carapace drops to 0.6%; flak now stops most autogun rounds (9% penetrate).
- **Weak Points and eye lenses** (user direction: massed fire should be able to hurt heavy armour, as in the lore). Every armour has a 3d Weak Points rating (halves DR on a failed-to-penetrate hit); eye-lens DR lowered (Astartes 50 → 40, carapace visors 20, Terminator 60); random face hits strike an eye on 1 in 6.
- **Astartes DX 16, Guardsman DX 12** (user direction). Astartes package gains DX +5 (543 points), Neophyte DX +3. Marine DX skills re-costed to hold their levels (core Guns (Rifle)-20, Broadsword-19). Guardsman 102 → 82 points.
- **Ork anchors added** (Boy ST 22/DX 11/HT 13/HP 26 up to Warboss ST 32/SM +1; Ork gear Malf 14, low Acc, crude armour Weak Points 7-8).
- **Astartes and Custodes Basic Move +4; Custodes DX 20** (user direction). Marine Move 12, Custodian Move 14. Custodes package DX +9 (655 points), DX skills re-costed to hold levels. Sister of Silence gains Guns (Rifle/Pistol); her Psyniscience removed (a Pariah can't sense the warp).
- **Enhanced Move (Ground)** (user direction: Marines and Custodes run very fast): Astartes 1/2 (sprint ×1.5, ~60 km/h), Custodes 1 (×2, ~90 km/h).
- **Scouts get Enhanced Move (Ground) 1/2** (user direction), same as full Astartes.
- **Orks: Thick Skull replaces Injury Tolerance (No Brain)** (user direction): DR 4 skull-only (6 points); headshots work normally once they penetrate.
- **Aeldari anchors added** (Guardian DX 14, Aspect Warrior DX 16, Exarch DX 18; shuriken 5d(3) cut; Aspect armour DR 70 flexible).
- **Necron anchors added** (natural necrodermis DR from Warrior 50 to Overlord 100; Reanimation Protocols; gauss flayer 6d(3) burn).
- **Gauss divisor (3) → (4)**: the anchor's own description (strips flak and carapace) needed (4); at (3) a gauss flayer got through carapace only 28% of the time.
- **Aeldari speed** (user direction): ancestry Basic Move +3 and Enhanced Move 1/2; Aspect Training a further Basic Move +2. Guardian Move 9, Aspect Warrior and Exarch Move 12.
- **Tyranid anchors added** (bioforms from Termagant to Carnifex; chitin DR 25-150; weapon-symbiotes as traits; synapse and Shadow in the Warp; rending claws).
- **Termagant symbiotes raised for flak 30**: fleshborer 6d(2) pi+, devourer 5d(2) pi++, spinefists 3d(2) pi+ (burrowing beetles and worms); at the original anchors Termagants could barely hurt flak-armoured Guardsmen.

## Combat simulator

- **New tool:** a Combat Simulator page on the site. Build two sides from any templates, adjust loadouts, and run hundreds of second-by-second GURPS fights (ranged and melee, Dodge and Parry, Deceptive Attack, hit locations and eye lenses, armour divisors, Weak Points, shields, wounding and Injury Tolerance, follow-ups, shock, stun, consciousness and death checks, Reanimation Protocols, morale). Default loadouts for all 80 templates are in `data/sim/loadouts.yaml`.
- **Site data:** weapon lines now carry their full default list, and template stats carry combat flags (High Pain Threshold, Hard to Kill, Combat Reflexes, Extra Attack, Injury Tolerance, poison resistance, Reanimation).
- **Simulator findings fixed:** the choppa is sw+1d(2) cut and the big choppa sw+2d(2) cut (the old sw+3 and sw+4 could not get through flak 30); the Carnifex's Brawling rises to 14. Engine: rending claws' "success by 5+" line now applies; Deceptive Attack is chosen to maximise the chance of landing a blow; automatic fire is spread across two or three models (B373) instead of piling onto one; a model in close combat fires its gun point-blank when that does more harm; Synapse Discipline counts as Fearlessness 5 and Necrons never break.
- **Lasgun recoil** is 2 on automatic fire (user direction) for every lasgun pattern.
- **Electrostatic gauntlets** (Corpuscarii) gain a weapon line, 7d(2) burn, Acc 3, 10/100, so GCS and the simulator can roll the arc; the Electro-Priest's simulator loadout uses them and the Voltagheist Field. Innate attacks with no ammunition ("-") no longer reload.
- **Reference fights:** `docs/sim-results.md` lists 75 matchups across every faction.
- **Injury Tolerance (Damage Reduction)** (Powers p. 53, 25 points per level of divisor) for tough organics: Ork Boyz ÷2, Nobz, Big Meks and Meganobz ÷3, Warboss ÷4 (Gretchin none); Tyranid Warriors, Raveners and Lictors ÷2, Carnifex and Hive Tyrant ÷3. The simulator divides injury after wounding.
- **Battlesuit structure:** XV25 +10 HP, XV8 +30, XV88 +40, as an HP bonus while the suit is worn. **Vespid chitin** DR 25 (Weak Points 7, eye 12); Vespid target 250–320.
- **Ork melee skills** raised (user direction): choppa 14 for Boyz and Kommandos, 15 for Nobz and Meganobz, 17 for the Warboss; Brawling 13 for Boyz, 14 for Nobz.
- **Simulator: Revised Fractional Health** (the user's house rule) as an alternative wound system: wound severity by injury as a fraction of HP, with each level's shock, stun and knockout rolls, brain, neck and break checks, Progressive Limb Trauma and impairment. How repeated wounds stack is not in the rules; it is a setting (default five boxes per level, from the Fractional Health 40k sheet).
- **Fractional Health boxes** (user rule): five boxes per level per location, and one hit marks as many boxes of its level as its injury reaches on the sheet's columns (×1, ×1.25, ×1.5, ×1.75 of the threshold; ×1, ×1.125, ×1.375, ×1.625 for Minor, as the sheet computes it), spilling into the next level when a row is full. Thresholds checked against every character on the Fractional Health 40k sheet.
- **Necrons under Fractional Health** (user direction): Unliving counts as ordinary Injury Tolerance, not the rules' full immunity, so Necrons and drones can be stunned and knocked out. The simulator also now reads the Necron "No Pain Receptors" trait as High Pain Threshold, which it is (it had been missed in both wound systems).

## Undecided fights

- **Why fights stalled:** the 60-second limit was too short for armoured grinds; fighters in close combat never reloaded an empty gun, so Marines ended up duelling with knives that couldn't penetrate; and a Hive Tyrant stood still for a minute waiting for its regrowing venom cannon.
- **Fixes:** a model in melee reloads when its gun beats its blade and the reload is quick (3 s or less); bio-weapons with long reloads regrow ammunition on their own while the creature fights; the limb injury cap now covers the follow-up as well as the bolt (B420); the default limit is 1,200 s; results separate "still fighting at the limit" from "both sides broken". No reference fight now times out under either wound system.

## Full GURPS combat engine

- **The simulator is rebuilt on a 2D hex map** (user direction): facing and arcs (flank −2, rear no defence), movement through free hexes, and an AI choosing among the Basic Set maneuvers (Aim with extra turns, Attack, Move and Attack, All-Out Attack Determined or Double, All-Out Defense, Feint, Rapid Strike, Ready, Change Posture, Concentrate). Attackers aim at the most damaging hit location (random locations remain an option). New: explosions splashing neighbours, fragmentation, cones, malfunctions and plasma overheating, Block and shield DB, Dodge and Drop, posture, cover (per side), crippling effects, knockback, bleeding, FP, and psychic attack powers with the Perils of the Warp table. A replay shows one battle on the map. Rules with page references: `docs/simulator.md`.
- **New simulator data:** `data/sim/weapons.yaml` (Malf ratings, overheat damage, cones, shield DB, blasts) and `data/sim/powers.yaml` (attack and defence powers for the Sanctioned Psyker, Zoanthrope, Farseer, Warlock, Weirdboy and Cryptek).
- **Called shots for elites only** (user direction): a model picks its hit location only if its template is 200+ points with IQ 8+; everyone else rolls random locations (still with the 1-in-6 eye-lens rule). "Everyone aims (RAW)" and "Random" remain settings. `docs/sim-results.md` now leads with the elite-aim tables.
- **Psykers get Innate Attack** (user direction) as the skill for their attack powers: Sanctioned Psyker 13 (169→173 points), Farseer 17 (622→630), Warlock 16 (358→362), Weirdboy 13 (497→501), Cryptek 15 (823→831); Zoanthrope already rolls 12. `data/sim/powers.yaml` uses these levels.
- **Imperial psychic attack powers rebuilt at lore scale** (user direction: lore scale, not party balance). Smite is now a warp-lightning Innate Attack (Burning) with Armor Divisor (10), 12 levels, 12d(10) burn, 162 points; Molten Beam 12d(10) burn, 162; Inferno 8d burn ex with Explosion level 2, 68; Crush 8d(2) cr Malediction, 88; Fire Bolt 12d burn, 42; Flame Breath 5d burn cone, 30; Assail 8d cr, 28. Trait `levels` now hold these lore levels as the default a trained battlefield psyker buys (lesser psykers buy fewer). Warp Empowerment grades re-mapped to levels 1-3 (Iota-Theta), 5 (Epsilon), 16 (Delta/Gamma, cap 170), 18 (Beta), 20 (Alpha), 25+ (Alpha-Plus). Sanctioned Psyker 173 → 550 (Empowerment 1 → 16), Librarian add-on 211 → 482 (Empowerment 3 → 20); Astropath and Grey Knights stay at Empowerment 3 (their powers need nothing more).
- **Xenos psychic attack powers rebuilt at lore scale:** Executioner 10d(10) imp with Malediction 2 (213 points), Eldritch Storm 8d(2) cr ex, Explosion level 3 (108), Destructor 7d(2) burn ex (62), 'Eadbanger 10d(10) cr with Malediction 2 (210), Warp Blast 20d(5) burn (220). Warp Empowerment follows the costliest power: Farseer 21 (1131 points), Warlock 6 (445), Weirdboy 20 (864), Zoanthrope 21 (798). Rune Armour's ward now grows 5 SP per Empowerment level above 2, capped at 160 (Warlock 80, Farseer 155).
- **Simulator:** Malediction follows B106 (a Quick Contest against the target's Will, no defence; −1/yard at level 1, normal range penalties at 2, none within 200 yd at 3); warp powers die against a blank (Pariah Gene); higher Explosion levels widen the burst; Flame Breath's cone is modelled; only piercing, impaling and tight-beam burning attacks may aim at the eye (B399). The Farseer and Sanctioned Psyker hold back and cast, and the Weirdboy walks up casting instead of charging.
- **Chinks in Armor** (B400, user direction) for aiming attackers (elites by default): −8 on the torso, −10 anywhere else, and the worn armour's DR is halved for that hit (natural DR is not; a chink hit doesn't also roll Weak Points). The planner weighs chinks against ordinary locations, so eye lenses (−9) usually win against helmeted foes and chinks show up against Ork Nobz, Aspect armour, and the skull or neck in melee.
- **Zoanthrope Warp Field** (user direction) 60 → 200 SP, recharge 10 → 25 SP/s (2 s delay, 4 s after collapse): above a storm shield, as the tabletop's long-running 3+ invulnerable save suggests. Priced at the trait's 0.5 points per SP, 30 → 100; Zoanthrope 798 → 868 points.
- **Simulator AI:** a shooter whose aimed roll would be below 8 closes the range instead of firing (a power that costs FP isn't wasted); a single-shot or low-RoF weapon re-aims when its unaimed roll is below 10 (Aim then Attack, B364); a model on the "advance" stance only fires on the move while the moving shot is 8 or better, and otherwise stands and shoots. Snipers and gauss weapons gained the most (Aeldari Rangers vs Guardsmen 62% → 98%, Immortals vs Marines 0% → 67%).
- **Mobs (user report: 50 charging Orks didn't swarm 5 Marines).** Melee movement now searches (breadth-first, through free hexes) for the nearest open hex beside any foe, so a mob flows round its own front rank and spreads over every enemy with room beside it; before, each model stepped greedily at the nearest foe and stopped when the hex ahead was full, leaving a queue. In the test fight Ork melee attacks per turn went from 2 to about 20.
- **Elite (called shots) is now skill-based:** best combat skill (weapon or power) 17+ and IQ 8+. The old 200-point rule made every Ork Boy an elite (ST 28 costs 300+ points) swinging for neck chinks at −10, while missing Skitarii and Sicarians. Elites now: Scions, Commissars, Battle Sisters, Inquisitors, Marines, Custodes, Sisters of Silence, Skitarii, Sicarians, Aeldari Rangers, Aspect Warriors and up, Kabalites, Wyches and the other Drukhari fighters, Deathmarks, the Warboss, Fire Warriors and up, Kroot, Vespid, the Lictor, the Farseer.
- **Expected-harm fixes:** random-location attackers judge their damage over the whole hit-location table (B552) instead of the torso only, and every estimate counts Weak Points. Before, Guardsmen "couldn't hurt" a Sicarian (torso DR 71) and ignored its DR 1 hands, and Ork Boyz went on All-Out Defense against Marines instead of swinging. A model on the charge stance never turtles.
- **Grappling** (B370–371, user direction: a mob should drag a Marine down). A charging model whose weapon can barely hurt its foe (under 1 HP expected per swing) grabs it instead: an attack with DX or Wrestling/Judo, defended normally. Holders then try a takedown (Quick Contest of the higher of ST, DX or grappling skill; the held model is at −4 DX) and, once it's down, a pin (Quick Contest of ST). Several grapplers pull together: the strongest one's ST plus a fifth of each other's (simulator rule). A held model can't retreat, parries at −4, attacks at −4, can't fire a gun and isn't knocked back; a model on the ground or pinned spends its turn trying to break free (Quick Contest of ST). A pinned model is helpless: no active defence, and any attacker may aim at it, chinks included. Two grapplers keep the pin; everyone else lays in with All-Out Attack (Strong, B365) and **Mighty Blows** (extra effort, 1 FP, B357), each +2 damage or +1 per die, or Determined when the hit matters more than the damage.
- **Result:** 50 Boyz charging 5 shooting Marines from 30 yards now win every fight and lose about 9 Boyz (was 0% before grappling); 30 Boyz also win; 20 Boyz break at half strength and lose. A lone Cryptek now loses to 5 Boyz (was 100% win).
- **Six more maneuvers** (user direction), rules in `docs/simulator.md`: **Wait** (B366; the shooter a dangerous charger is heading for holds fire and shoots it as it closes, and a charger dropped there makes no attack); **suppression fire** (B409; RoF 5+ weapons spray a 3-yard zone over a cluster, attacking everyone in it or entering it at 6 + rapid-fire bonus); **grenades** (B410; lore-standard frag, krak, stikkbombs and plasma grenades added to 12 loadouts, range from ST and weight, Ready then throw); **Slam** (B371; chargers that can't cut their foe crash into it, HP × velocity ÷ 100 dice each, knockdown on double damage or a failed DX roll); **Shove** (B372); **two weapons at once** (B417; blade and pistol at −4 each, off hand −4 more without Ambidexterity, which is now a combat flag); **Feverish Defense** (B357; 1 FP for +2 against a dangerous blow).
- **Fixes found testing them:** a model killed mid-move by suppression fire or a Wait shot no longer keeps moving (it crashed some big fights); a creature whose regrowing bio-weapon (a natural weapon, not a gun with a long reload) is empty now closes to melee instead of standing idle (the Hive Tyrant vs 5 Marines under Fractional Health was timing out 72% of the time).
- **Custodes plate fields** (user direction): Auramite and Allarus armour each carry an integrated energy field, the source of the Custodians' 4+ invulnerable save in the lore, built with the Iron Halo's numbers (120 SP, 3 s delay, 20 SP/s). Framework field table and the Custodian Guardian and Shield-Captain loadouts updated. 3 Custodians vs 10 Marines goes from 27% to about 95%.
- **Dodging grenades and blast damage** (user direction): the target of a thrown grenade may Dodge it like any thrown weapon (B377), and a dodged grenade goes off a yard away; anyone else within 3 yards may dive for cover (a Dodge that puts them a yard further off, prone). Only the model struck directly faces an explosive's armour divisor; the blast around it has none (B414). This applies to every explosive weapon, so krak grenades and plasma splash no longer cut through neighbours' armour.
- **Fire distribution** (user report: Marines weren't using grenades on Orks). Grenades weren't the problem: a frag grenade barely hurts a Boy (about 14 injury per fragment against HP 32 and Damage Reduction), so the AI rightly shoots instead. The problem was overkill: every Marine aimed at and shot the same Ork, then spent a turn aiming again at the next. Shooters now discount foes their squad-mates are already shooting, and aim only when the aimed roll hits over 1.8 times as often as a shot now. 5 Marines vs 20 charging Boyz: 72% → 98%; against 30 or 50 Boyz they now take 10–12 Orks with them (was 2–7).
- **Parrying grabs and punches with a weapon** (B376, user question): the attacker's arm takes the weapon's damage. A Marine's chainsword parry cripples a grabbing Boy's arm.
- **Lychguard fight with their warscythes** (user report: they weren't hitting). The simulator loadout gave them the hyperphase sword and dispersion shield, which use Broadsword, a skill the template doesn't buy, so they swung at a default of 12; the template trains Two-Handed Sword 16 for the warscythe, which the loadout now uses (10d−1(10) cut, reach 1–2). Also fixed: the "can't hurt it, so grab or slam it" test used the chance to land a wound, which folds in the foe's parry, so skilled swordsmen facing a Parry-17 Custodian switched to grappling. It now asks only whether the weapon gets through armour when it lands.
